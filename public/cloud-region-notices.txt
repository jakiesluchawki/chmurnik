CHMURNIK: on-device cloud-region proposals (experimental)

These two models locate areas for the user to inspect. They do not identify
cloud genera, cloud altitude, flight conditions or individual cloud boundaries.
No user photograph or private feedback was included in their training data.

CloudMaskV4Research
Architecture and training: CHMURNIK, MobileNetV3-Small encoder and compact decoder.
Whole-image RGB input at 256x256; normalization inside the graph. Float32 Core ML.
Training data: All-Sky Imager Cloud Segmentation Dataset Almeria, v1.0.1.
Authors: Yann Fabel, David Magiera, Bijan Nouri, Niklas Blum, Luis F. Zarzalejo.
Organizations: DLR and Research Centre for Energy, Environment and Technology.
Source: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16647156
Data license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: images resized to 256x256; cloud categories merged into one binary
mask; ignored pixels excluded from the loss. Capture days and duplicates
separated across training, validation and test. Dataset images are not bundled.
